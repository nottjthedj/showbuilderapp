Prompt Sheet

The whole film broken into clips of 15 seconds or less — one idea and one camera move each. Nothing is condensed to fit the limit; the film is cut into more pieces instead. — clips, — of generated footage for a film that cuts to about nine minutes. That surplus is the point: you cut on the beat rather than using whatever the model handed you.

What the Rico test showed

The look is right — suit, moustache, scar, medallion, tiger, mirrored pink room. The problem is timing, and it's visible frame by frame.

The turn happens between two frames

5.30s · profile5.47s · profile 5.63s · FACING LENS5.80s · facing lens

That isn't a turn — it's an internal jump cut. He is in profile with a cigar at 5.47s and full-face down the lens 0.17 seconds later. There's no rotation, no eye-line travel, no moment of being noticed. The audience never gets the beat where the character catches them watching, which is the entire point of the fourth wall.

The cause isn't the model. One 15-second prompt was asked for four things — establish the room, light a cigar, turn to camera, deliver a rant. Given four beats and 15 seconds, it will snap through the one that has no dialogue attached. That's always the turn.

Three other things in the same clip

    The fix — every turn is three clips

    Roughly 30–35 seconds of material for the beat that was trying to happen in under a second. Generate all three, cut them together, and the turn finally lands.

    1 · Approach

    Still in the scene. He stops what he's doing and registers that you're there — but says nothing yet. Quiet. This is the clip that was missing entirely.

    10–12s · camera locked

    2 · The turn ◆

    Static locked camera. The rotation of the head is the only motion in frame, and the only instruction in the prompt. Never combine it with a push-in — the model drops one, and it drops the turn.

    15s · camera locked, no drift

    3 · The hold

    He finishes the line and holds the look while the game card lands over him. Gives you an out point that isn't mid-syllable.

    8–10s · camera locked
