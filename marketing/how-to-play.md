# How to Play — the explainer video

**Runtime:** 75–90 seconds. **Format:** 16:9 for the site and YouTube, 9:16 cutdown for social.
**Voice:** TJ, to camera, present-day (not the 1977–83 film look — this is him talking to you now).

This video has one job: **kill the objection.** The single biggest reason people don't buy a
ticket to an interactive night is the fear of being dragged on stage. Answer that early and
answer it flatly.

---

## The script

**[0:00–0:06 · HOOK — TJ to camera, hard cut in, no logo]**

> Nobody's getting pulled on stage. Nobody's doing a dance-off. You can stay exactly where you
> are with a drink in your hand and still win the entire night.
>
> Here's how this works.

**[0:06–0:18 · STEP ONE — pick a colour]**
*On screen: four wristbands, four colours, four crowd pockets lighting up.*

> You get in, and before anything else you pick a colour. Gold, pink, cyan or violet.
>
> That's your crew for the night. Four gangs are in that building and every one of them is
> short on people — so from the second you're through the door, you're one of theirs.
>
> Pick with your friends or pick against them. Both are more fun than you think.

**[0:18–0:32 · STEP TWO — the story plays]**
*On screen: the film on the big screens, the room watching, a character turning to camera.*

> Through the night, the story plays on the screens. It's a film. Four crews, one vault, and
> the worst night of everybody's life.
>
> And every so often it stops — and whoever's on screen turns around and looks straight down
> the lens at you, and hands you a job.

**[0:32–0:50 · STEP THREE — phone up]**
*On screen: a game card hitting the screens, then a whole floor of phones lighting up at once.*

> That's your cue. Phones up.
>
> The game opens on every phone in the building at the same time and you play it right there,
> on the floor, against every other crew in the room.
>
> There's nothing to download at the door. Nothing to carry. No props, no queue, no getting
> picked. If you can hold a phone and hear me, you're playing.

**[0:50–1:05 · THE STAKES]**
*On screen: the leaderboard flipping, four colours climbing.*

> Twelve games across the night. Every one of them scores for your colour, and the board is up
> the whole time so you can watch your crew win — or watch your crew get embarrassed by a
> table of strangers in violet.
>
> And at the end of the night, it all comes down to one vault and one four-colour code.

**[1:05–1:20 · THE CLOSE]**
*On screen: TJ, then the doors, then black.*

> That's it. That's the whole thing. Turn up, pick a colour, keep your phone charged.
>
> Two rules. Don't get wasted. Don't get busted.
>
> Everything else, I'll walk you through on the night.

---

## Production notes

- **The objection goes first.** Do not build to it. The first six seconds must say *nobody gets
  pulled on stage* — that's the line that converts.
- **Show a real floor.** The single most persuasive shot in this video is a wide of hundreds of
  phones lighting up at the same moment. Get that shot; it does more work than any line here.
- **Burned-in captions throughout.** This gets watched on mute on a phone in a queue.
- **No spoilers.** The vault reveal and the man with no face never appear. This video explains
  the *mechanic*, never the ending.
- **Cutdowns:** the 0:00–0:18 chunk stands alone as a 20-second social post; the 0:32–0:50
  "phone up" chunk stands alone as the reply you paste whenever someone asks how it works.
