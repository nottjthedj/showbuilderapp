## How to assemble it

1. Lay the beats down in this order and do not reorder them. The run of show is locked to the app — the film and the games fire in sequence, so a beat moved in the edit is a beat moved in the room.
2. Assemble at these lengths first, then loosen. It is far easier to let a good clip breathe than to find thirty seconds you don't have on the night.
3. The APPROACH → TURN join is the one cut that has to be invisible. Match the head angle across it and the turn reads as one continuous move — that join is the whole fix.
4. Never trim the head of a TURN. The rotation is the shot; losing its first frames is exactly the failure the three-clip structure exists to prevent.
5. Game cards go on in post, over the last clip of the beat. Nothing is generated with text in it, so the card is yours to time.
6. Cut picture first, then drop the recorded voices, then the music bed. The beat's music note says where to duck and where the drop lands.
